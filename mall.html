Century City Walk Planner

A shopping district split into four blocks by streets, modeled loosely on Westfield Century City in Los Angeles. You can only cross at crosswalks, and every block has its own garage. Should you walk to the next store, or move your car to a closer garage?

Straight line Walking Driving

Click a store to go there. Click a garage (P1 to P4) to park there. Shift-click a store, or click a shopper, to set where you are now. The layout is simplified for teaching. Store and garage positions are illustrative, not the real floor plan. One cell is 10 m.

Your trip

Walk or move the car?

The totals include walking back to your car at the end, since you have to return to it either way. Assumes walking at 80 m/min, driving at 300 m/min, plus 4 min to leave one garage and park in the next.

Which garage is closest on foot to each store?

Walking distance in meters from each garage’s elevator to the store entrance. The highlighted cell is the best place to park. Click a row to plan a trip to that store.

Close in a straight line, far on foot

    Store pairs with the biggest gap between the straight line and the actual walk. These are usually stores across a street from each other with no crosswalk nearby.
